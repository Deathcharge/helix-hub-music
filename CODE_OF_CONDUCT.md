# Samsarix community code of conduct

Samsarix LLC is committed to a welcoming, respectful project community. Participation in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project spaces requires professional and inclusive conduct.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect and assume good intent while addressing real impact.
- Welcome different backgrounds, identities, experience levels, and communication styles.
- Keep criticism specific to ideas, behavior, and code.
- Give credit, protect private information, and accept correction constructively.
- Help keep technical conversations focused and accessible.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, threats, intimidation, doxxing, deliberate disruption, sustained personal attacks, and misuse of maintainer authority are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in official Samsarix Workspace community spaces and when someone publicly represents the project. Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant when necessary to protect the community. Responses will consider context, impact, pattern, and severity.

Report conduct concerns privately to [support@samsarix.com](mailto:support@samsarix.com). Include relevant links, dates, and context. Reports will be handled as confidentially as a fair investigation allows. Do not use public issues for sensitive reports.

Good-faith reports and appeals will not be punished. If the report concerns a person who would normally handle it, Samsarix LLC will assign a different reviewer where possible.

This policy draws on the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) and the [Python Community Code of Conduct](https://policies.python.org/python.org/code-of-conduct/).
